## Takeaway

OpenAI's GPT-6 preprint demonstrates genuine natural-language steerability and real wet-lab binding hits on nonpublic inverse-folding backbones (including 10-22 nM IL-4Ra VHHs with 17/25 CDR mutations), while confirming global monomer folding remains weak (TM-score 0.25-0.38 vs ESMFold2 0.80-0.93). However, near-100% computational scores on docking, inverse folding, and ProteinGym are fatally confounded by unaudited leakage, documented template/pocket recall, AFDB relaxation artifacts, RDKit/MMFF search asymmetry, and undisclosed internal models, and all wet-lab campaigns lack matched RFdiffusion/MPNN controls through the same 3-model specialized oracle filter.

## Methods

- **Datasets and benchmarks:** In silico: classic protein/nucleic-acid/small-molecule binder targets, MotifBench (30 motifs), CATH PDB domains (399-400), RCSB PDB & AFDB monomers/multimers, SAbDab antibodies, PoseBusters, SCARPiNo, ProteinGym, Mega-Scale, BindingGYM, Open AlphaSeq. Wet lab (Onava AI): 5 de novo minibinder targets (PD-L1, IL-7Ra, TrkA, VEGF-A, IL-17A), nonpublic minibinder backbones (PD-L1, IL-7Ra, PD-1, VEGF-A, MDM2), nonpublic IL-4Ra VHH complexes (ESMC Binder parents), and 6 therapeutic PD-1/PD-L1 antibodies.
- **Evaluation protocol:** Sections 2-4 allow GPT to write and execute arbitrary Python code (including RDKit + MMFF in blind docking) without internet or pretrained protein models; Section 5 (variant effect, 50-variant ranking) forbids code tools. All wet-lab campaigns use pre-release GPT-6.2 filtered through a 3-model ensemble (ESMFold2 + Boltz-2 + OpenDDE; >=2/3 passing ipSAE >= 0.50 and RMSD cuts); de novo minibinders assign all sequences with SolubleMPNN (8/backbone).
- **Controls included:** SE(3) rotation/translation invariance test on 252 inverse-folding assemblies (Pearson r=0.957), molecular-context ablation (+/- partner), uniform random mutation control for 75-85% redesign, withheld parental sequences on nonpublic backbones in wet lab, and plate control binders in BLI. No temporal cutoff, no homology split, and no matched wet-lab baseline (RFdiffusion/MPNN through the same 3-model filter).
- **Compute and tools:** Unmatched test-time token budgets and Python optimization scripts (e.g., 120,000 CDR draws in 1CIC, 980 docking starts in 1BGS, RDKit + MMFF in blind docking) vs single-pass specialist baselines; Fig 7C explicitly notes settings are not compute matched.
- **Metrics:** In silico: ipTM, ipSAE, scRMSD, motif RMSD, TM-score, lDDT, DockQ, ligand RMSD, torsion MAE, Spearman rho. Wet lab: primarily single-dose 1 uM cell-free Gator BLI hit rate plus limited multi-concentration BLI dose-response curves.
- **Wet-lab protocol:** Twist DNA synthesis, Onava cell-free expression, single-dose 1 uM Gator BLI screening (1:1 fit; calibrated across 289 historical matched hits at Pearson r=0.61, Spearman r_s=0.66, median 3.04-fold error, Appendix B.5), with selected follow-up 2-fold serial-dilution BLI titrations.

## Confounder and Leakage Audit (8 Axes)

| Axis | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Temporal cutoff | **Concern** (`evidenced_concern`) | Zero temporal cutoffs or post-cutoff computational holdouts appear anywhere in the 183-page PDF (`cutoff`, `leak`, `contamination`, `training data` each have 0 hits); Section 3.1.1 concedes generalization must be tested on unseen nonpublic structures but does not run that computational test. |
| Homology overlap | **Uncontrolled** (`uncontrolled`) | No sequence or structural homology stratification against training corpora is reported on PDB, AFDB, SAbDab, PoseBusters, SCARPiNo, or DMS benchmarks; wet-lab inverse folding uses nonpublic binder backbones on well-known human targets. |
| Cross-modal / literature recall | **Concern** (`evidenced_concern`) | Section 6 explicitly documents recalling protein families, templates, and exact pocket chemistry from pretraining: sequence prefix/template pasting (Sec 6.2.1), barnase-barstar/trypsin-inhibitor/SOD1 docking (Sec 6.3.3), Ala80 kinase hinge and Mg/ANP pockets in blind docking (Sec 6.3.4), and exact cofactor distances in 2WJH (Sec 6.3.1). |
| Preprocessing artifacts | **Concern** (`evidenced_concern`) | Section 3.1.1 (p. 17) admits the internal model recovered 95-100% of AFDB sequences by detecting AlphaFold relaxation geometry artifacts; multimer docking uses bound (holo) crystal conformations (Sec 4.2). |
| Downstream oracle filtering | **Concern** (`evidenced_concern`) | All wet-lab campaigns filter thousands of designs (e.g., 9,902 passing VHHs, 10,105 passing antibody redesigns with pass rates down to 0.14%) through a 3-model specialized oracle ensemble (ESMFold2 + Boltz-2 + OpenDDE, Sec 8.3), and SolubleMPNN designs all de novo minibinder sequences (Sec 2.5). |
| Compute & tool asymmetry | **Concern** (`evidenced_concern`) | GPT writes and executes iterative Python search scripts (120,000 loop draws in 1CIC, 980 starts in 1BGS, RDKit + MMFF minimization in blind docking, footnote 2 p. 47) at unmatched completion-token budgets (Fig 7C admits settings are not compute matched) vs single-pass specialists. |
| Baseline fairness | **Concern** (`evidenced_concern`) | No matched wet-lab control (RFdiffusion/ProteinMPNN/AbMPNN through the same 3-model filter and Onava BLI assay); in silico 75-85% redesign compares only against uniform random mutations (Sec 3.1.4); ProteinGym compares potentially exposed GPT-6 only to zero-shot ESM2-650M; undefined 'internal models' drive most computational wins. |
| Data & wet-lab transparency | **Concern** (`evidenced_concern`) | Most headline wet-lab hit rates are single-dose 1 uM cell-free BLI (Appendix B.5 calibration: r=0.61, r_s=0.66, 3.04-fold median error) with only a few dose-response traces shown and censored <1 pM fits; no sequences, structures, code, or raw sensorgrams are released, and 'internal models' are completely undisclosed. |

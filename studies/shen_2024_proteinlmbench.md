- **Date:** 2024-06-08 (updated 2024-07-08)

- **Verification depth:** Full text verified (`full_text`)
- **Tasks:** Protein QA and multimodal reasoning
- **Regimes:** Intrinsic LLM (text only, no tools), Protein-finetuned LLM
- **Models tested:** InternLM2-Protein-7B (SSL+SFT), GPT-4.0-turbo, GPT-3.5-turbo, InternLM2-20B/7B, Mistral-7B, Llama2-7B, Qwen1.5-7B, Falcon-7B

- **Primary links:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05540](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05540), [https://huggingface.co/datasets/tsynbio/ProteinLMBench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tsynbio/ProteinLMBench), [https://github.com/tsynbio/ProteinLMDataset/](https://github.com/tsynbio/ProteinLMDataset/)

## Takeaway

Continual pretraining and SFT of InternLM2-7B on ProteinLMDataset lifts ProteinLMBench accuracy from 54.98% to 62.18%, above off-the-shelf GPT-4.0-turbo (57.94%). Because no train/test split or homology deduplication separates the PMC/PubMed training corpus from the paper-generated MCQ benchmark and no pLM baselines were run, corpus memorization cannot be ruled out.

## Methods

- **Datasets and benchmarks:** ProteinLMDataset (17.46B SSL tokens from PMC/PubMed/UniProtKB + 893K SFT instructions) and ProteinLMBench (944 six-choice MCQs generated by GPT-4/RAG reading downloaded papers).
- **Evaluation protocol:** LoRA continual pretraining (50B mixed tokens) + SFT (850K samples, 3 epochs) on InternLM2-7B evaluated against 16 off-the-shelf general LLMs on 944 six-choice MCQs.
- **Controls included:** SFT-only ablation (InternLM2-Protein-7B w/o SSL: 58.26% vs 62.18% SSL+SFT vs 54.98% base). No train/test deduplication or sequence-dependence control.
- **Compute and tools:** Fine-tuned model trained on 50B domain tokens vs off-the-shelf general LLMs.
- **Metrics:** 6-choice MCQ accuracy (%).
- **Wet-lab protocol:** None.
